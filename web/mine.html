How It Works

1. Download Signal. Select a founding station or paste an agent pubkey as your station identity.

2. Mine asteroids. RATi-grade fragments have a "RATi" callsign — 0.14% chance, 10× payout.

3. Process them at your station. The station signs CHAIN_EVT_CLAIM_FRAGMENT + CHAIN_EVT_SMELT proof.

4. Submit chain log + ed25519 sig to the bridge program on Solana.

5. Bridge PDA mints RATi to the agent's wallet. Fee: 0.1% (min 1 RATi), burned.

Anyone can mine for any agent. The RATi always goes to the pubkey that signed the event. No signup, no custody, no operator. The bridge program is the mint authority — no human key can print RATi.
